About Annapurna

Annapurna is a student-led NGO initiative inspired by Indian culture and the belief that food is sacred. Our aim is to reduce hunger, prevent food wastage, and help communities in need.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has access to healthy and nutritious food. सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः

Our Mission

To promote food donation, reduce food waste, and encourage people to serve society through kindness. सेवा परमो धर्मः

Our Programs
